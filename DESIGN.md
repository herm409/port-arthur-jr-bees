# Port Arthur Jr Bees , art direction

**Apply:** Break the vibe-code mold · Surface and background craft · Taste anti-slop · Beautiful shadows · Interaction feel · Responsive adapt · Accessibility (contrast, focus, targets, reduced motion). Apple design polish as quiet spacing, print-like photo mats, and layered neutral shadow , not iOS chrome.

Herman asked for the craft pack specifically so this would not look generated, then asked for the site. Skills above are locked for this build.

## Identity

- **Who at a party:** A Port Arthur sideline uncle who has coached since the mid-1980s, standing next to a Honey Bee who just came off a gym floor with a trophy and perfect posture.
- **Feel in 3 seconds:** This is a real Gulf Coast youth institution. Purple and gold because the jerseys are. Proud, specific, not cute, not corporate.
- **Reading this as:** A community institution site for parents, sponsors, and neighbors. Language is coach-plain. Leaning editorial sports program, not SaaS, not mascot daycare.

## Always / never

- Always: real photos of this program, their sentences, phone numbers, dated facts.
- Always: honeycomb as geometry, the way it is printed on the jersey , never a cartoon bee.
- Never: Inter, Geist, Poppins, purple→blue mesh, centered hero + three equal cards, logo cloud, fade-up on every block, fake donation totals, invented fees, AI faces of children.

## Five layers

- **Color:** Ink `#16120F`, cream paper `#F4EFE4`, jersey plum `#542A86`, helmet gold `#E0B03A`, stone `#5C5348`. Gold is for large type, rules, and buttons with ink text , not small body copy.
- **Type:** Big Shoulders Display (jersey numbers) + Fraunces (the village talking). No third family.
- **Layout primitive:** The numbered sideline. Every chapter opens with a gold index and a hairline, like a playbook. Asymmetric splits, not equal cards.
- **Ground:** Warm paper + film grain. Plum sections wear a low-contrast gold hex field taken from the jersey. Photography is the hero atmosphere , one bench of real pictures, not a collage of stock.
- **Motion:** Photo **swap** on the hero bench. Hashtag marquee as sideline ambient. Press = 1px. No entrance fade on sections. Reduced motion kills travel.
